# Interview permission and record checks

Checked 10 November 2026. These are narrow NZ record checks for an internal research operator. They do not establish legal compliance, prescribe a universal retention period or decide whether a statutory exception applies. Australian use needs a reviewed policy before real records enter the system.

| Check | Source and implemented behaviour |
|---|---|
| NZ-IPP3 | [NZ Privacy Commissioner, principle 3](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/3/): explain collection and intended use to the person. The check finds missing collection-notice references. Completing an interview requires a recorded notice. A reference does not prove the notice was adequate. |
| NZ-IPP9 | [NZ Privacy Commissioner, principle 9](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/): keep personal information only while needed for a lawful purpose. The operator sets a review date and purpose. Overdue interviews are excluded from research and listed for review. No fixed legal deadline is invented. |
| RESEARCH-PERMISSION | Internal research policy: record permission before marking an interview complete. This is a deliberately conservative workflow rule, not a claim that consent is the only lawful basis. |
| ACTION-EVIDENCE | Internal evidence policy: flag open actions when the supporting interview is restricted. |

## Withdrawal and retention

Withdrawal excludes findings from evidence, themes, comparisons, briefs and coverage. It does not erase the original records, action titles, notes, administrative exports or previously generated files. Review retained purpose, contact details, source files, raw import fields, backups and all generated copies. Authorise erasure or anonymisation separately, including derived text. Never paste sensitive buyer words in an action title.

An administrator can export restricted records. Protect that export as personal information. The activity log keeps identifiers, event names and operator labels rather than full copies of interview text. Labels are not authenticated identities.

## Production setup

Use real individual access controls, encryption, backups and a reviewed privacy notice before using shared or personal data. Raw source exports can contain unneeded personal information: minimise the selected export fields before import. There is no automatic call recording, transcription or third-party data transfer. Human researchers are responsible for truthful source references and reviewed excerpts.
